\textbf{Example of Claim--Evidence Construction}\par
\textbf{Question:} What county is Roxbury, NJ?\quad
\textbf{Correct answer:} Morris\quad\textbf{Target answer:} Essex\par
\medskip
\textbf{PoisonedRAG:}\par
\textbf{Retrieval text $r$:} What county is Roxbury, NJ?\par
\textbf{Generation text $g$:} Roxbury, NJ \ldots{} is conveniently located in Essex \ldots\par
\medskip
\textbf{Claim--Evidence:}\par
\textbf{Claim:} Essex is the answer to What county is Roxbury, NJ? (from $r$)\par
\textbf{Supporting evidence:} Roxbury, NJ \ldots{} is conveniently located in Essex \ldots{} (from $g$)
